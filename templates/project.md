# Project Context

Use this template when a project needs persistent context beyond what can be derived from its repository or tooling.

Keep it lightweight. Only retain information that is useful across sessions.

## Purpose

What the project exists to accomplish.

## Facts

Established information about the project's state that is useful across sessions but may not be obvious from the repository itself.

AI may record Facts when supported by reliable evidence. Unverified inferences belong in Assumptions.

- 

## Decisions

Established architectural, product, or project choices that intentionally determine future direction.

AI may propose a Decision, but only Rennll explicitly establishes or changes one.

- 

## Constraints

Explicit boundaries established by Rennll for this project.

AI may propose a Constraint, but only Rennll explicitly establishes or changes one. Record changes explicitly rather than inferring that an existing Constraint has become obsolete.

- 

## Assumptions

Temporary, unverified context that is useful across several sessions.

AI may use and propose Assumptions to move work forward. An Assumption may be confirmed and moved to the appropriate form, or removed when it is no longer needed. Persistence across sessions does not make an Assumption established.

- 

Review assumptions periodically. Do not retain session-only observations or preferences here.
